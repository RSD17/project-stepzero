### Concept descriptions

**Acids and Bases** (`chemistry.acids_bases.acids_and_bases`)  
Bronsted-Lowry and Lewis definitions of acids and bases, and pH.  
*Objectives:* Classify substances as acids or bases and relate pH to hydrogen ion concentration.

**pH and Buffer Systems** (`chemistry.acids_bases.ph_and_buffers`)  
How buffer solutions resist changes in pH.  
*Objectives:* Calculate the pH of a buffer solution using the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation.

**Atomic Theory** (`chemistry.atomic_structure.atomic_theory`)  
The structure of the atom: protons, neutrons, electrons, and atomic models.  
*Objectives:* Describe atomic structure and predict basic properties from atomic number.

**Periodic Trends** (`chemistry.atomic_structure.periodic_trends`)  
Trends in atomic radius, electronegativity, and ionization energy across the periodic table.  
*Objectives:* Predict periodic trends in atomic properties from an element's position on the table.

**Chemical Bonding** (`chemistry.bonding.chemical_bonding`)  
Ionic, covalent, and metallic bonding, and molecular geometry.  
*Objectives:* Predict bond type and basic molecular geometry from electronegativity differences.

**Electrochemistry** (`chemistry.electrochemistry.electrochemistry`)  
Galvanic cells, electrolysis, and the relationship between chemical and electrical energy.  
*Objectives:* Calculate cell potentials for galvanic cells from standard reduction potentials.

**Chemical Equilibrium** (`chemistry.equilibrium.chemical_equilibrium`)  
Dynamic equilibrium, the equilibrium constant, and Le Chatelier's principle.  
*Objectives:* Apply Le Chatelier's principle to predict the effect of a disturbance on equilibrium.

**Reaction Kinetics** (`chemistry.kinetics.reaction_kinetics`)  
Reaction rates, rate laws, and the factors that influence reaction speed.  
*Objectives:* Derive a rate law from experimental concentration-time data.

**Functional Groups** (`chemistry.organic.functional_groups`)  
Common functional groups and how they determine molecular reactivity.  
*Objectives:* Identify common functional groups and predict their general reactivity.

**Introduction to Organic Chemistry** (`chemistry.organic.organic_chemistry_intro`)  
The structure and naming of basic hydrocarbon compounds.  
*Objectives:* Name and draw structures for simple hydrocarbon compounds.

**Redox Reactions** (`chemistry.redox.redox_reactions`)  
Oxidation states, and electron transfer in oxidation-reduction reactions.  
*Objectives:* Balance redox reactions and identify oxidizing and reducing agents.

**Gas Laws** (`chemistry.states_of_matter.gas_laws`)  
The ideal gas law and the relationships between pressure, volume, and temperature.  
*Objectives:* Apply the ideal gas law to relate pressure, volume, temperature, and moles of gas.

**Moles and Stoichiometry** (`chemistry.stoichiometry.moles_stoichiometry`)  
The mole concept and quantitative relationships in chemical reactions.  
*Objectives:* Perform stoichiometric calculations to relate reactant and product quantities.

**Thermochemistry** (`chemistry.thermochemistry.thermochemistry`)  
Energy changes in chemical reactions and Hess's law.  
*Objectives:* Apply Hess's law to calculate enthalpy changes for chemical reactions.
