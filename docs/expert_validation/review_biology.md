### Concept descriptions

**Biomolecules** (`biology.biochemistry.biomolecules`)  
The structure and function of carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, and nucleic acids.  
*Objectives:* Relate the structure of each major biomolecule class to its biological function.

**Cell Structure** (`biology.cellular.cell_structure`)  
The organelles and structures of prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells.  
*Objectives:* Identify the major organelles of a eukaryotic cell and describe their functions.

**Cellular Respiration** (`biology.cellular.cellular_respiration`)  
How cells convert glucose into usable chemical energy (ATP).  
*Objectives:* Trace the flow of electrons and energy through cellular respiration.

**Membrane Transport** (`biology.cellular.membrane_transport`)  
Passive and active transport of substances across the cell membrane.  
*Objectives:* Distinguish between passive and active transport mechanisms.

**Photosynthesis** (`biology.cellular.photosynthesis`)  
How plants convert light energy into chemical energy.  
*Objectives:* Describe the light-dependent and light-independent reactions of photosynthesis.

**Ecology Basics** (`biology.ecology.ecology_basics`)  
Populations, communities, and energy flow through ecosystems.  
*Objectives:* Describe energy flow and nutrient cycling within an ecosystem.

**Natural Selection** (`biology.evolution.natural_selection`)  
The mechanism by which populations evolve over generations.  
*Objectives:* Explain how natural selection acts on heritable variation within a population.

**DNA Replication** (`biology.genetics.dna_replication`)  
How DNA is copied prior to cell division.  
*Objectives:* Describe the semiconservative mechanism of DNA replication.

**DNA Structure** (`biology.genetics.dna_structure`)  
The double helix structure of DNA and its role as genetic material.  
*Objectives:* Describe the structure of DNA and explain how it encodes genetic information.

**Gene Expression** (`biology.genetics.gene_expression`)  
Transcription and translation, the process of turning genes into proteins.  
*Objectives:* Trace the flow of genetic information from DNA through mRNA to protein.

**Mendelian Genetics** (`biology.genetics.mendelian_genetics`)  
Patterns of inheritance discovered by Gregor Mendel.  
*Objectives:* Apply Punnett squares to predict inheritance patterns for simple traits.

**Population Genetics** (`biology.genetics.population_genetics`)  
How allele frequencies change in populations over time.  
*Objectives:* Apply the Hardy-Weinberg principle to compute allele and genotype frequencies.

**Homeostasis** (`biology.physiology.homeostasis`)  
How organisms maintain a stable internal environment.  
*Objectives:* Describe feedback mechanisms that maintain physiological homeostasis.

**Introduction to Human Physiology** (`biology.physiology.human_physiology_intro`)  
An overview of the major organ systems and how they interact.  
*Objectives:* Describe how the major organ systems work together to maintain body function.
