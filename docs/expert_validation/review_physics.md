### Concept descriptions

**Electric Circuits** (`physics.electromagnetism.circuits`)  
Current, voltage, resistance, and basic circuit analysis.  
*Objectives:* Analyze simple series and parallel resistive circuits.

**Electromagnetic Induction** (`physics.electromagnetism.electromagnetic_induction`)  
Faraday's law and the generation of electric current from changing magnetic fields.  
*Objectives:* Apply Faraday's law to calculate induced EMF in changing magnetic fields.

**Electrostatics** (`physics.electromagnetism.electrostatics`)  
Electric charge, electric fields, and Coulomb's law.  
*Objectives:* Apply Coulomb's law and the concept of electric field to point charges.

**Kinematics** (`physics.mechanics.kinematics`)  
The description of motion in terms of position, velocity, and acceleration.  
*Objectives:* Derive and apply the equations of motion for constant acceleration.

**Momentum and Collisions** (`physics.mechanics.momentum`)  
Linear momentum, impulse, and conservation of momentum in collisions.  
*Objectives:* Apply conservation of momentum to elastic and inelastic collisions.

**Newton's Laws of Motion** (`physics.mechanics.newtons_laws`)  
The three laws relating force, mass, and acceleration.  
*Objectives:* Apply Newton's three laws to analyze forces on simple systems.

**Orbital Mechanics** (`physics.mechanics.orbital_mechanics`)  
Gravitational orbits, Kepler's laws, and satellite motion.  
*Objectives:* Derive orbital parameters from gravitational force and Kepler's laws.

**Rotational Motion** (`physics.mechanics.rotational_motion`)  
Angular velocity, torque, and rotational analogs of linear motion.  
*Objectives:* Apply torque and angular momentum concepts to rotating rigid bodies.

**Work and Energy** (`physics.mechanics.work_energy`)  
Work, kinetic energy, potential energy, and conservation of energy.  
*Objectives:* Apply the work-energy theorem and conservation of energy to solve problems.

**Introduction to Quantum Mechanics** (`physics.modern_physics.quantum_mechanics_intro`)  
Wave-particle duality, the Schrodinger equation, and quantization of observables.  
*Objectives:* Interpret the wavefunction and apply the Schrodinger equation to simple systems.

**Special Relativity** (`physics.modern_physics.special_relativity`)  
Time dilation, length contraction, and the postulates of special relativity.  
*Objectives:* Apply the postulates of special relativity to compute time dilation and length contraction.

**Introduction to Statistical Mechanics** (`physics.thermodynamics.statistical_mechanics_intro`)  
Connecting microscopic particle behavior to macroscopic thermodynamic quantities.  
*Objectives:* Relate probability distributions of microstates to macroscopic temperature and entropy.

**Thermodynamics Basics** (`physics.thermodynamics.thermodynamics_basics`)  
Temperature, heat, and the first and second laws of thermodynamics.  
*Objectives:* Apply the first law of thermodynamics to simple thermal systems.

**Optics** (`physics.waves.optics`)  
Reflection, refraction, and basic wave behavior of light.  
*Objectives:* Apply the laws of reflection and refraction to simple optical systems.

**Oscillations and Waves** (`physics.waves.oscillations_waves`)  
Simple harmonic motion and the basic properties of mechanical waves.  
*Objectives:* Model simple harmonic motion and describe basic wave properties.
